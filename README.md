### ZombieGame
A 2D game made in OpenGL where you fight incoming hordes of zombies

When running .\ZombieShooter.exe, the user is greeted by a start screen containing the commands for playing the shooter. To start playing, just press 'enter'.

<img width="798" height="621" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/300d3f3a-bbbf-49a9-83d9-98ee19565d0a" />

The player has 3 lives. For each enemy that crosses the line, they lose 1 life. Lives are represented by the 3 red bars in the upper right corner.
The score in the upper right corner represents the number of enemies killed, and the counter resets with each new game.

<img width="798" height="631" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2013b69c-8c83-49d8-bd1b-561827058c30" />

The player can shoot at enemies at a limited rate of at most 2 shots per second. Each shot that hits an enemy takes away 1/3 of its health bar. After 3 shots, the enemy's health reaches zero and it disappears.

<img width="798" height="631" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0b517885-e496-465c-a154-2874b471976c" />





After 3 enemies cross the white line, the player's number of lives reaches 0 and the game ends.
